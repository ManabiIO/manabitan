Decision: no additional concrete correctness blocker found in the reviewed source/lifetime boundary. This is a read-only source review, not a new execution or whole-import qualification.

1. Publication and completion are distinct. The first owner's early publication occurs only after its resolved content references and canonical metadata are published, in-flight exact sources are registered, and its record append has been started. Its content/record operations remain joined by allSettled. An eligible successor starts after that publication but its beforePublication waits for predecessor.settled. Thus successor metadata and record writes cannot overtake predecessor completion. Branches without early reservation publish only at terminal completion.

2. Rollback reverses begun dependencies. The predecessor records its successor only after publication has been observed and before the successor touches mutable DB state. Both content and record promises are drained before the DB's outer catch asks beforeRollback. That method latches cancellation, rejects predecessor.settled/publication first, then joins the successor's terminal completion. A successor waiting beforePublication wakes, drains its own started content work, rolls back its reservation/plan, and completes before predecessor rollback. The same method's second use in the coordinator error continuation is idempotent with respect to these barriers. This avoids the predecessor-completion/successor-publication wait cycle. Already-started failures remain owned, including undefined rejection values and terminal AggregateErrors.

3. Serial callers use the same queue. Active-journal bulkAddArtifactTermsChunk enters appendSerial; it cannot bypass a captured drain or leapfrog an eligible predecessor. A serial owner is canOverlap=false, so its following owner also waits for full completion. The coordinator deliberately rejects a third simultaneous admitted call instead of creating an unbounded queue. The normal producer holds at most two; this explicit admission contract must remain documented if other callers are introduced.

4. Producer lead and byte budgets retire at completion. The parser captures the non-consuming owned witness before the DB cache can consume it. An owner retains its chunk and queuedOwnedContentBytes until retireSink. nextSinkGroupIndex and lead-source activation advance only after actual completion. A private, borrowed, unwitnessed or empty result drains the pending predecessor before progressing. No third sink callback starts while two owners remain.

5. Borrowed leases drain independently of peer-parser failure. Active sink consume callbacks are removed from the queued-result consumers set; _fail can release unused queued results without releasing bytes an active sink may still read. The catch path joins every acquired completion before releasing active owners and then joins worker loops. The DB's existing early borrowed-source release remains idempotent and occurs after its content reader completion; lookup/record columns are separately owned. Sequential parser wrappers explicitly await completion tickets.

6. Source clearing and metrics are completion-owned. Importer publication signals do not clear columns. processTermChunk awaits the ticket completion, or drains completion on publication failure, before normal clearing/progress/metric accumulation. Per-ticket metrics avoid reading another in-flight chunk's global metrics. The read-wall subtraction uses the union of active sink intervals, not their overlapping sum.

7. Finalization repair preserves lifecycle ownership. finish and abort claim FINALIZING before closing admission/draining, and retain the same journal/session identity until drainage ends. New calls fail admission; previously admitted contexts may finish in FINALIZING while transaction/session ownership remains valid. Late append failures enter the existing finish rollback/finally path. close and purge claim exclusive cleanup before drainage. The no-pipeline close returns synchronously, preserving the original cleanup scheduling. Session.finalize/dispose promises also block new beginArtifactTermChunk calls immediately.

8. Activation is narrow. Database overlap requires enabled content dedup, initialized codec/live block session, JMdict codec selection, a complete witnessed whole shared source, no borrowed release callback, complete row-to-unique metadata, and the current session owner. Importer publication tickets are used only without media requirements. Private/unwitnessed/partial/media inputs retain completion-awaiting behavior. The 4-worker cursor rotation changes only shared-span worker assignment, not block bytes, result order, metadata snapshots or failure drainage.

Limitations: no broad tests or new timing were run. The executor disconnected during later read-only queries; remaining source was fetched by exact remote commit. Existing generic asynchronous mutation contracts and the two-owner admission bound are not expanded by this review. Hosted validation and whole-import performance/memory gates remain separate requirements.

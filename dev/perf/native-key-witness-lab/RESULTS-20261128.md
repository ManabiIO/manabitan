# Early native key witness: rejected experiment

The candidate did not establish a JMdict improvement and is not a product PR. Its second independent JMdict cohort and Jitendex cohort regressed parser-plus-lookup CPU. All observed real-corpus outputs were byte-identical to the baseline. The structural idea also overlaps a separate concurrent owner's existing experiment, identified after this run began.

- Baseline: `9e10d3b3fcf66d344ab31bc42c9b522fa5215da2`.

## Invalid synthetic negative controls

All three original synthetic fixtures used two 30,000-row banks. Existing native per-bank sizing forced every measured parse onto the fused fallback: four fallbacks for four repetitions in every one of their 108 observations. Their timings therefore cannot qualify a unique-key miss-path claim. The JSON preserves them without filtering. Their A/C noise also exceeded the intended screen tolerance. A six-bank, 10,000-row fixture correction was prepared prospectively but was not launched after the candidate failed and ownership overlap was found.

## Earlier local work and overlap

The original [local-attempts.json](https://github.com/ManabiIO/manabitan/blob/b00e6c899270ac636a397945a19cd0825bdc6b5d/dev/perf/native-key-witness-lab/local-attempts.json) preserves the substantially slower local JMdict candidate, incomplete VNDB execution (exit 137), missing generated-bundle setup failures and the later successful 349-test run. Those recovered local summaries are not represented as complete raw evidence and are not pooled with the hosted cohort. The workspace executor became unavailable, which is why the exact source patch and tests were recovered into the laboratory branch before fresh CI validation.

A different concurrent owner already has [product 23781611da3b3c86f8524ac774487692490c4040](https://github.com/ManabiIO/manabitan/commit/23781611da3b3c86f8524ac774487692490c4040) and [study run 46101039947](https://github.com/ManabiIO/manabitan/actions/runs/46101039947). Both variants test moving the complete bounded adjacent raw-key equality proof before strict parsing on the same baseline. That owner packs the witness into existing mask bits; this rejected candidate uses a stack witness struct. No duplicate PR or follow-up browser study is planned for this candidate.

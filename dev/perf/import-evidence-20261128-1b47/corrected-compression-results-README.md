# Corrected compression trial: complete results and final disposition

The fixed corrected trial supports **closing the shared Zstd −2 candidate without
a product PR**. Both complete-import cohorts had five of six candidate pairs
slower, with positive paired-median and equal-work changes. JMdict passed the
registered semantic, exact-storage and sampled-memory gates. Jitendex passed the
semantic and exact-storage gates but failed the separate memory-coverage gate.
Neither corpus established the useful complete-import signal required by the
prospectively frozen review rule.

These are unfavorable observations and a predeclared review decision, not proof
of a general causal latency regression: both candidate descriptive intervals
cross zero. No pair, unfavorable tail, failed gate, null memory aggregate, or
original artifact member was selected out of the retained non-package evidence.
The earlier warm compression-component benefit does not override these completed
browser observations.

This packet contains the corrected study's validation evidence and **both complete
fixed browser cohorts**, including **624 original non-package browser members,
52 raw report/proof sets, and all 547 raw memory-scan rows**. The 13 original
validation artifact members and its complete original ZIP are also retained.
There are no new observations, variants, replacement cohorts, retries, or
reused old-trial warmups in this preservation step.

## Post-outcome phase context is separate from the decision

At root's request after the Jitendex primary result, a separate read-only analysis
examined the same six measured pairs and two identical-code controls. It adds no
observation, confidence interval, endpoint, gate, or alternative decision rule.
Its exact records, descriptive helper, source timer boundaries, and independent
review are retained under the Jitendex review snapshot.

That context found a compression-owner paired median of −4.17% and equal-work
change of −3.48%, while the identical-code control median was −2.41%. Inclusive
content persistence moved −3.10% by median and −3.61% by equal work. At the same
time, source inflation was higher in every candidate pair, and bulk finalization
had a longer tail, including the retained 135→364 ms and 134→201 ms observations.
The complete original phase table retains all movements, not just these examples.

The compression-owner timer spans awaited batch completion, including dispatch,
readiness, and worker-return effects; it is not isolated codec CPU. Content
persistence includes that work and may overlap record append. The append
residual excludes separately timed reference generation and is not a syscall-only
storage timer. Worker inflate/parser metrics have other scopes. These values
must not be added or subtracted to reconstruct import time, remove unfavorable
tails, or claim that the compression change caused the other phase movement.
No JMdict phase expansion was started. This contextual analysis changes neither
the primary results nor the no-PR disposition.
